# English word bank: sources and licenses

`vocab_bank.json` is generated by `tool/english/build_vocab_bank.py`. It is a
derived dataset, distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/),
because two of its inputs require share-alike.

## Inputs

| Input | What it gives | License |
|---|---|---|
| CEFR-J Vocabulary Profile 1.5 (A1–B2) | headwords | Free for research and commercial use, with citation. Copyright Tono Laboratory, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. |
| Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 1.0 | headwords | CC BY-SA 4.0, Octanove Labs |
| wordfreq 3.1.1 (Robyn Speer) | frequency ranking; filters for the invented words | Code: Apache-2.0. Data: CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Both profiles were taken from
[openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj](https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj)
at commit `d4e45b75b38f27b30dfc5c44d8c571aec7e7092f`.

## Citations

- The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5. Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of
  Foreign Studies. http://www.cefr-j.org/download.html
- Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2, version 1.0. Octanove Labs.
- Robyn Speer. wordfreq, version 3.1.1. https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq

## What is NOT from these sources

The invented words (`pseudowords`) are generated by the script from a letter
model of the bank itself. They are not copied from LexTALE, X-Lex or any other
published test.

The CEFR level the app reports comes from the vocabulary-size table in Milton &
Alexiou (2009). Only its thresholds are used, in code, with the citation next to
them. No data from that paper is in this file.
