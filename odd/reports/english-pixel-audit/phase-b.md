The device lands on Android launcher instead of reading/highlighting text or displaying a recoverable error. Reentering the app and repeating on `Money` yielded the same exit; after the second, `pidof com.lifeos.lifeos` was empty. Evidence: `article-loading.png`/`.xml` → `reader-play.png`/`.xml`; `article-retry.png`/`.xml` → `reader-play-retry.png`/`.xml`. This is an observed failure; no crash stack, codec/voice diagnosis, or acoustic quality is claimed. App-scoped logcat (`--uid=10308`) returned no matching events in this session. The earlier Phase A listening button's unexpected navigation remains inconclusive, not merged with this finding.
