A later batch captured valid XML by streaming `exec-out uiautomator dump /dev/tty`, trimming its trailing diagnostic after `</hierarchy>`, and parsing the result. `transport-ui.xml` is valid but describes Android SystemUI, not LifeOS. Never infer accessibility/semantics from `home.xml` or SystemUI.

## Restoration, test records, and limitations

- Original goal was `Trabajo y clientes`; briefly tested `Vida diaria` and `Viajes`, then restored and visually verified. Voice, model, OS settings, volume and network were not changed. The reminder picker was cancelled without creating an alarm. No install, reset, user-history deletion, publication or unrelated app operation occurred.
- Started one **partial** vocabulary retake and exited after two answers; the hub still showed A1. Started listening placement, submitted one **blank** first prompt at 0%, advanced to Oración 2 and did not submit it. Persistence of either in-flight attempt/score beyond displayed state is unverified; these are test actions, not silently erased. Prior recordings were only viewed.
- Device frequently returned all-black screenshot captures or ignored an input when the display slept; wake/toggle and recapture were required. An attempted second Listen capture unexpectedly showed Android's LifeOS App info; we used its Back control to return to the launcher and relaunched the same app. Cause is unproven; do not infer a LifeOS crash or a successful second Listen. `restarted.png` was overwritten with a valid but black screencap after an earlier `am start` stdout contaminated it.
- No secure authentication appeared. Actual audio quality, two-play limit, full listening completion, full vocabulary retake result, reminder save/reversal, positive daily-plan completion, and deeper speech/writing flows remain **NOT RUN**. Direct hub reader/review navigation and the listening keyboard were exercised in the second batch; see below and `phase-b.md`. This is an incomplete audit, **not** complete English feature certification.
