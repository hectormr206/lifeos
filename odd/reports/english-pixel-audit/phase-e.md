## Key Learnings

1. A saved Real Talk rephrase creates an immediately visible FSRS review card and swaps its own button to `Guardada para repasar`; it does not require contacting a real person. Blank/whitespace conversation controls remain enabled no-ops in two distinct observed practice contexts, but no explicit product contract has been established for calling them a defect.
2. This batch's English `16:37` reminder appeared in global `Recordatorios` and was removed through `Acciones → Eliminar`; picker text `04:37` mapped to visible `16:37` with no AM/PM control in the captured text-mode UI. Restoring absence was verified for **this Phase E entry**. Phase F's separate `05:43→17:43` entry did not appear in the global list, so its cleanup is unresolved; see `phase-f.md`.
3. The selected Spanish voice was Daniela, not an assumed generic Axi ID; English Lessac US was installed but not selected. Bounded preview, read-aloud, archive and neighbor FLACs each contained nonzero device output without native exit; these different signal checks must not be promoted to unsampled speech-quality claims.
4. A short quiet read-aloud recording produced literal `[silence]` and 0%, and Next advanced to a nonblank sentence. Positive recognition and ZIPA coaching remain dependent on an actual physical voiced source, not typed practice or synthetic playback.
