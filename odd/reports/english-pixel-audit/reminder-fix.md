# English reminder picker and global-list source fix

## Reproduction and change

Phase F TESTPixel showed a 24-hour-looking Spanish picker displaying `05:43` without AM/PM, followed by a saved English reminder at `17:43`. The app resolves Spanish devices to the concrete `es` locale (`mobile/lib/l10n/locale_providers.dart`); Flutter's Spanish Material localization uses `H_colon_mm`, but its input parser in `/opt/buildenv/flutter/packages/flutter/lib/src/material/time_picker.dart` (`_parseHour`, around lines 1775–1797) branches on `MediaQuery.alwaysUse24HourFormat` alone. With that flag false, the initial 20:00 PM survives the edit as an invisible period. The English reminder picker now wraps **only its dialog** in a MediaQuery whose parsing flag matches `hourFormat` of the actual Material-localized display. An already-true 24-hour setting remains true; English 12-hour AM/PM remains visible and functional. No SDK, global OS setting, app-wide locale, scheduler or repository write semantics changed.

The global local-reminders notifier survives route exit; English creates through the same service but does not refresh the notifier. The global screen now refreshes on each entry **after** the notifier's initial `ready` load and checks `mounted` before starting the reload. This avoids an older initial load overwriting a newer refresh and avoids launching a late refresh from an already-disposed route. It does not modify deletion, graph persistence, scheduling or English readback. The separate device recovery in `reminder-recovery.md` subsequently confirmed the exact Phase F `17:43` reminder became visible after pull-to-refresh and only that entry was deleted through the UI. Global and English views were then observed empty/restored on revisits; native AlarmManager absence was not queried.
