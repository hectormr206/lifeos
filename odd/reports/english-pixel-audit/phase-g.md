## Key Learnings

1. The candidate2 installed base APK is byte-for-byte hash-identical to the staged signed release, despite intentional version-code reuse; launcher and retained review-card content survived `install -r`.
2. In the actual Spanish TESTPixel picker, typed `05:43` persisted as morning `5:43`, and typed `17:44` persisted as `17:44`. After first opening global reminders empty, each creation appeared there automatically without pull-to-refresh, and targeted deletion restored both screens.
3. Empty/whitespace roleplay actions and independent Real Talk situation/wanted-to-say actions now gate correctly in the sampled workflows. One slow Work model reply was observed after the initial interim snapshot; a quick Real Talk rephrase completed before its busy state could be sampled.
4. Reader playback, selected Daniela preview and one listening sentence produced distinct nonzero bounded audio while the new PID survived; these are sampled digital-output and control-flow checks, not unsampled quality or real microphone evidence.
